Here is what it means

This idea is known as Maslow's Hammer or the Law of the Instrument.

It means people tend to use the one tool or skill they know best — and believe it can solve all the problems.

Examples:

  • A programmer solves every problem with Python, even when SQL would be simpler.
  • A lawyer sees every disagreement as something to litigate.
  • A manager thinks every issue needs another meeting.
  • An engineer wants to automate everything.
  • A politician believes every problem needs another law.

The "hammer" represents our favorite solution.
