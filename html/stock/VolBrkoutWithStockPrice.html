Volume Breakout with Price Movement

This report scans for stocks showing abnormal volume surges combined with positive price action — a classic sign of institutional interest and potential strong momentum. High volume on up days often indicates the start of a new uptrend or continuation of an existing one, making these stocks attractive for swing trades and short-to-medium term gains.

Column Explanations

  • stock code: The ticker symbol or identifier of the stock (e.g., RELIANCE, TSLA, DBS). This is how you can quickly find and track the stock on your trading platform.
  • current price: The most recent trading price of the stock (real-time or last close depending on market hours). This is your reference point for entry and target calculations.
  • last return pct: The percentage price change over the most recent period (typically the last trading day or session). Positive values (especially >2–5%) show strong upward price momentum on the breakout day.
  • volume ratio: The ratio of today's (or latest session's) volume compared to the average volume over a lookback period (commonly 10–50 days). Values > 1.5–3.0x indicate unusually high trading activity — the key signal in volume breakouts.
  • volume cr: Cumulative Relative Volume or "Volume Confirmation Ratio" — often shows how much the volume has accumulated above average over recent sessions (e.g., last 3–5 days). Higher values reinforce that the volume surge is sustained, not just a one-day spike.
  • last close: The closing price of the previous trading session. Useful for calculating intraday movement and comparing with current price.

Tips for Picking Stocks for Strong Moves (Swing / 2–8 Weeks Horizon)

Volume breakouts with positive price action are among the most powerful setups for quick and explosive returns. Focus on these key filters when scanning the report:

Priority What to Look For Why It Matters Typical Expected Move
1 (Most Important) volume ratio ≥ 2.0x – 4.0x (ideally >3x) Shows serious buying interest from institutions/smart money High probability of 10–30%+ follow-through
2 last return pct ≥ +3% to +8% (preferably +4–10%) Confirms the volume is accompanied by strong bullish price action Stronger the candle → stronger the trend continuation
3 volume cr elevated (e.g., >1.5–2.0 over last few days) Indicates sustained interest, not just a one-day wonder Reduces false breakout risk
Avoid / Caution volume ratio <1.5x OR last return pct negative/flat Lack of conviction — likely a failed or weak breakout High risk of reversal or chop

Best setups: volume ratio > 3× + last return pct > 5% + breakout above key resistance.
These frequently deliver fast 12–40% moves in 3–8 weeks during bull markets or strong sectors.
Always confirm with chart patterns (e.g., consolidation breakout), support levels, and stop-loss below breakout day low.
