Gap Up with Follow-Through

This report highlights stocks that opened significantly higher than the previous close (gap up) and then continued to move even higher during the session. A gap up with strong follow-through buying is one of the most bullish short-term patterns, often driven by positive news, earnings surprises, or institutional accumulation — signaling potential for powerful multi-day or multi-week uptrends.

Column Explanations

  • Stock code: The ticker symbol or identifier of the stock (e.g., SEA, AMD, OCBC). Use this to quickly pull up charts and trade the stock.
  • current price: The most recent trading price (real-time or latest available). This is your current reference for the stock's momentum.
  • open price: The opening price of the current trading session — the level where the gap occurred.
  • previous close: The closing price from the prior trading session. This is the baseline used to calculate the gap.
  • gap up pct: The size of the gap up, calculated as [(open price - previous close) / previous close] × 100. Larger gaps (≥ 4–8%) tend to be more significant when accompanied by follow-through.
  • intraday gain pct: The percentage gain from the open price to the current price during the session. Positive values show follow-through strength — the higher, the better.
  • volume cr: Cumulative Relative Volume (or volume confirmation ratio) — measures how much today's volume has exceeded the average (often over 10–20 days). Elevated values confirm that the move is supported by real buying interest.

Tips for Picking Stocks for Strong Moves (1–4 Week Horizon)

Gap ups with follow-through are explosive setups. Focus on these key filters when scanning the report:

Priority What to Look For Why It Matters Typical Reward Potential
1 (Most Important) gap up pct ≥ 5–12% (ideally 6–10%+) Significant gaps show conviction. Small gaps (<4%) often fade. 10–40%+ multi-day/week moves
2 intraday gain pct ≥ +2–6% (strong follow-through) Proves buyers are stepping in aggressively after the gap — key to continuation Stronger the follow-through → higher probability of multi-day run
3 volume cr ≥ 2.0x – 5.0x (ideally >3x) High volume validates the move as institutional, not retail noise Reduces chance of gap-fill reversal
Avoid / Caution gap up pct < 4% OR intraday gain pct < +1% OR low volume Weak gaps or fading moves frequently reverse or consolidate Higher failure rate

Power setup: gap up pct > 7% + intraday gain pct > 4% + volume cr > 3× + breakout above recent resistance.
These often produce fast 15–50%+ gains in 1–4 weeks, especially in bull markets or hot sectors.
Always: Confirm with catalyst (news/earnings), place stop-loss below open price or gap area, and watch for volume drying up as a warning sign.
