Composite Score - Multi-Factor Analysis

This report ranks stocks using a composite score that combines multiple technical factors into a single powerful metric. By blending trend strength, momentum, price position relative to moving averages, and volume activity, it helps identify the highest-conviction opportunities — stocks with the best overall setup for near-to-medium term gains. Higher composite scores indicate stronger alignment across indicators.

Column Explanations

  • stock code: The ticker symbol or identifier of the stock (e.g., GRAB, META, UOB). Use this to quickly access detailed charts and execute trades.
  • current price: The latest available trading price (real-time or most recent close). This serves as your reference for potential entry levels and targets.
  • pct above ma20: Percentage by which the current price is above the 20-day Simple Moving Average. Positive values show bullish positioning; higher % indicates stronger trend strength and momentum continuation potential.
  • momentum 10 d: The 10-day rate-of-change or momentum score (typically price change % over the last 10 trading days). Positive and high values reflect accelerating upward price action — a key driver of short-term outperformance.
  • volume ratio: Today's (or latest session's) volume divided by the average volume over a lookback period (often 10–50 days). Values >1.5–3.0x signal unusual buying interest, often confirming the strength of the other factors.
  • composite score: A proprietary or weighted score that combines the above factors (and possibly others) into one number. Higher scores = better overall setup. This is the primary sorting/ranking column — focus on the top values first.

Tips for Picking Stocks for Strong Moves (1–3 Month Horizon)

The composite score is designed to surface the highest-probability trades. Use these practical guidelines when scanning:

Priority What to Look For Why It Matters Typical Expected Move
1 (Most Important) composite score in the top 10–20% (highest values) The score already weighs multiple factors — top scores have the best confluence 15–50%+ potential in 4–12 weeks
2 pct above ma20 ≥ +5–15% AND positive Shows healthy distance from the short-term trend line — room to run without immediate overextension Strong trend continuation
3 momentum 10 d ≥ +8–20% Recent acceleration confirms buyers are in control Boosts probability of explosive follow-through
4 volume ratio ≥ 1.8–4.0x Volume validates the move — look for confirmation of institutional participation Reduces false signal risk
Avoid / Caution Low composite score OR negative momentum OR volume ratio <1.2x Lack of alignment across factors = lower conviction Higher chance of underperformance or whipsaw

Power setup: Top composite score + pct above ma20 > 8% + momentum 10d > 12% + volume ratio > 2.5×.
These stocks frequently deliver the strongest and cleanest 20–60%+ moves in 1–3 months during favorable markets.
Always: Cross-check the individual chart for clean patterns, use stop-losses (often below recent swing low or 20-day MA), and prioritize names in strong sectors or with catalysts.
