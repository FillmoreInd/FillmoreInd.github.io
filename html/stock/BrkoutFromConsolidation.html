Breakout from Consolidation

This report identifies stocks that have recently broken out of a tight, sideways consolidation range — a powerful setup in technical analysis. After a period of low volatility and range-bound trading, a decisive move above resistance on increasing volume often signals the start of a new strong uptrend. These breakouts are favorites among swing and momentum traders for their high reward-to-risk potential.

Column Explanations

  • Stock code: The ticker symbol or identifier of the stock (e.g., TCS, NVDA, UOB). Use this to locate the stock quickly on your charting platform or broker.
  • current price: The latest available trading price (real-time or most recent close). This is your current entry reference point.
  • resistance 10 day: The highest price level the stock reached in the last 10 trading days. This is typically the upper boundary of the recent consolidation range — breaking above it is the key breakout trigger.
  • support 10 day: The lowest price level in the last 10 trading days. This marks the lower boundary of the consolidation range. The tighter the support-to-resistance range, the more explosive the breakout tends to be.
  • consolidation range pct: The percentage width of the recent 10-day trading range. Calculated as [(resistance 10 day - support 10 day) / support 10 day] × 100. Lower values (e.g., < 8–12%) indicate a very tight, coiled consolidation — the most favorable for powerful breakouts.
  • breakout pct: How far the current price has moved above the 10-day resistance level, expressed as a percentage. Positive values confirm the breakout strength. Higher values show stronger conviction.

Tips for Picking Stocks for Strong Moves (1–3 Month Horizon)

The best consolidation breakouts deliver fast and significant gains. Prioritize these filters when reviewing the report:

Priority What to Look For Why It Matters Typical Reward Potential
1 (Most Important) consolidation range pct ≤ 8–12% (ideally < 8%) Tight ranges = coiled spring. Low volatility periods often precede the biggest moves. 15–50%+ in 4–12 weeks
2 breakout pct ≥ +1.5% to +5% (fresh breakout); ideally +2–4% Shows the breakout is real and has some follow-through momentum Stronger initial thrust = higher probability of continuation
3 Current price clearly > resistance 10 day Confirms the breakout has occurred (avoid stocks still testing resistance) Reduces false breakout risk
Avoid / Caution consolidation range pct > 15–18% OR breakout pct < +0.5% Wide ranges = less energy; tiny breakouts = likely fakeout or weak move Higher failure rate

Ideal setup: consolidation range pct < 8% + breakout pct > 2.5% + breakout on high volume.
These frequently produce explosive 20–60%+ moves in 1–3 months during favorable market conditions.
Always: Check for volume confirmation, place stop-loss below the breakout day low or 10-day support, and prefer breakouts in strong sectors/themes.
